### Motivation of the Archive

This project seeks to document oral histories of Puerto Rican communities and their stregies of survival after hurricanes, earthquakes and the COVID-19 pandemic. To make it as accesible as possible our collections are hosted using Wax a minimal computing infrastructure for ease of access and portability.



### Project Team

**Dr. Rosa Ficek**
, the project director, will coordinate the project’s day-to-day activities,
track progress, and evaluate outcomes. She will also lead efforts to integrate community input
into the project and document these processes. Dr. Ficek is a cultural anthropologist whose
research focuses on the relationships between technology, infrastructure and society;
participatory research methods; and disaster studies. She has 20 years of experience with
ethnographic research and a successful record of project completion with grant funds from
the National Endowment for the Humanities, the Mellon Foundation, and other organizations.
She holds a Ph.D. in anthropology from the University of California-Santa Cruz.

**Ana Rivera**  is the librarian at the UPR Cayey’s Victor M. Pons Gil library responsible
for the Hector Campos Parsi Collection on Images, Sound, and Movement. She has over 20
years of experience with cataloguing historical, audiovisual, and sound resources, research
and documentation, and collections development.

**Hilda Teresa Ayala** Archivist and librarian Hilda Teresa Ayala is an external
consultant to the project. Previously serving as
the Director of Puerto Rico’s General Archive, she has led initiatives to digitize archival
materials, improve environmental conditions for collections, and develop strategies for
prevention, preservation and emergency response. She holds a Master’s in Library and Information Sciences from the University of
Puerto Rico, a Master’s in Archival Studies from the University of British Columbia, and a
BA in Hispanic Studies from the University of Puerto Rico at Cayey

**Jose Hernandez** is the Digital Humanities Technologies Specialist at Florida State
University, who will join the project as a consultant. He holds a BA & MA in Digital Sudies of Language, Culture & History.
